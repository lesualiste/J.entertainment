Who are we?

J.entertainment is a French virtual entertainment agency created in January 2024.

In 2026, the agency decided to start from scratch with a new concept, new groups and new projects.

Our values

Our goal

Our goal is to create a safe and creative environment where artists can have fun, develop their talents and bring their projects to life.

Our story

J.entertainment began in January 2024. After several projects, the agency chose to restart its activities in 2026 with a fresh concept and new ambitions.
